Description:
Databricks' AI operations agent. It monitors the jobs, pipelines and tables you enroll, investigates failures, and proposes a fix you can verify.

## Definition
Genie ZeroOps opens an incident when an enrolled job fails or a table looks wrong, investigates the root cause, and proposes a fix you can check before it ships.

## How it fits with Genie One
Genie One is the front door: it answers "what's broken, who's affected, is MuleSoft down?" across the platform and links you onward. ZeroOps is the specialist for the deep root cause and the code fix. Genie One gives you the ZeroOps link for a failed run.

## Where to find it
Jobs & Pipelines, in the ZeroOps banner at the top of the page. Job owners add jobs with Configure.

## Today (Private Preview)
Incidents appear in the inbox of whoever enrolled the jobs. Notifications and shared team visibility are planned next; an API for reading incidents is planned later.
